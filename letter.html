The Letter

To you, my love.

This page is an envelope I never found the courage to send in person.

Unfolding the letter…

“Some days you just want someone to look at you and see everything you are.”

I didn’t know how to tell you this face-to-face. Not because I didn’t want to — but because some things feel bigger in writing. They settle. They wait for you to come back to them.

You make ordinary days feel like they’re happening inside a story I want to keep turning. From the way you laugh at your own jokes to the quiet, certain ways you show up, I’m paying attention. I always have.

You don’t have to try with me. You can be exactly as you are — loud in the kitchen, quiet on the couch, completely yourself — and I’ll still be here, holding the door. You’ve made my life softer and brighter just by being in it.

I could send you a hundred birthday gifts and none of them would say it the way this does. Not the flowers, not the cards, not the things that fade. The truth is the only thing that lasts, and this is my truth.

Thank you for being you — for the way you are in this world and in my chest. I hope you know how much you are loved, how deeply, and for as long as I’m here to say it. I hope this day is as warm and bright as you are.

I love you,
Yours,
[Your Name]
