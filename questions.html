Questions

I asked you a few things.

The ones I didn’t say out loud.

Cutie, I’ve been carrying a lot of things I never said in person. On this page, I’ve tried to say them — and each one has an answer to go with it. Tap a question and it opens up like an envelope.

1. Have you ever thought that I’m the only person who actually looks at you like you’re something?

I think about that every single day. That’s the only compliment I need.

2. What’s something about me you’re still surprised by?

How kind you are when you’re tired. That’s the one I never stop respecting.

3. If I had to describe you in three words, what would they be?

Inconsistent, unpredictable, and impossible not to love.

4. What’s a memory from us that you still replay in your head?

The day I finally worked up the courage to tell you. I’m still a little scared it worked out.

5. What do you want me to know about how you feel when I’m not around?

I miss you more than I say. But I’m always coming back.

6. What’s something I do that makes you feel safe?

The way I listen. Even when I don’t say anything at all.

7. If we were a song, what would we be?

Something slow that I’m never ready to end.

8. What’s your favourite thing about our ordinary days?

The ones where nothing dramatic happens and I just get to be with you.
